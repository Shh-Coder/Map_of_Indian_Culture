Tribal Culture

Andaman & Nicobar Islands are home to indigenous tribes such as the Jarawas, Sentinelese, and Nicobarese. Tribal traditions include unique dances, songs, and rituals passed down through generations.

These traditions preserve the folklore, stories, and heritage of the island communities.

Festivals

The islands celebrate a mix of tribal, local, and pan-Indian festivals, including Island Day and harvest festivals. Folk songs, dances, and community gatherings reflect the vibrant island lifestyle.

Festivals are an important way to keep tribal folklore alive among island communities.

Handicrafts & Art

Local artisans create bamboo crafts, shell jewelry, and traditional carvings. Many items depict tribal myths, folklore, and stories inspired by the islands’ natural beauty.

These handicrafts are sold in local markets and help preserve the cultural identity of the islands.

Heritage & Nature

The islands host historic sites like Cellular Jail and tribal villages, surrounded by lush forests and beaches. These sites, combined with local storytelling, keep the islands’ folklore and history alive.

Visitors can experience cultural performances, storytelling sessions, and guided heritage tours.

Culture & Folklore Summary

Andaman & Nicobar Islands boast a unique blend of tribal culture, folklore, festivals, and handicrafts. Indigenous traditions, dances, and storytelling keep the cultural heritage vibrant while connecting locals and visitors with the islands’ rich past.
