Ras Lila Dance

Ras Lila is the most famous classical dance form of Manipur, inspired by the love story of Radha and Lord Krishna. The graceful movements and devotional music make it spiritually enriching.

The dancers wear elaborate traditional costumes with cylindrical skirts and delicate embroidery that enhance the beauty of the performance. It reflects the deep religious and artistic culture of Manipur.

Loktak Lake

Loktak Lake is the largest freshwater lake in Northeast India and is famous for its floating islands called phumdis. It supports rich biodiversity and scenic beauty.

The lake is also home to Keibul Lamjao National Park, the only floating national park in the world, making it a unique ecological treasure of India.

Sangai Festival

The Sangai Festival is the biggest cultural festival of Manipur, celebrated annually to promote tourism and tradition. It showcases dance, music, art, and local cuisine.

Named after the rare Sangai deer, the festival highlights the cultural richness and vibrant heritage of the Manipuri people.
